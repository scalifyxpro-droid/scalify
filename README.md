# Scalify — A Digital Marketing Company

Global Programmatic SEO & Performance Marketing Architecture covering **50 Countries, 500 Cities, and 31 Digital Marketing Services** (yielding **15,500 localized programmatic landing pages**).

---

## 🚀 Key Highlights

* **15,500 Programmatic City Landing Pages:** `/[country]/[city]/[service]` (e.g. `/united-states/austin/meta-ads`, `/united-kingdom/manchester/google-ads`)
* **31 Service Pillar Pages:** `/services/[service]` (e.g. `/services/meta-ads`, `/services/seo-audits`)
* **50 Country Hub Pages:** `/countries/[country]` (e.g. `/countries/united-states`, `/countries/germany`)
* **Content Engine:** 2,000+ words per landing page with ~5% targeted keyword density, tailored per city, country, local currency, and market competition tier.
* **Direct Lead Capture:** Integrated WhatsApp conversion routing to `+91 6353583148` with pre-filled service and location details.
* **Mobile-First UX:** Sticky mobile WhatsApp action bar, responsive comparison tables, and FAQ schema structured data (JSON-LD).

---

## 🛠 Tech Stack

* **Framework:** [Astro 4.16](https://astro.build/) (Static Site Generation / SSG)
* **Styling:** Mobile-first Vanilla CSS with Google Fonts (*Playfair Display* & *Work Sans*)
* **Data Sources:** 
  * `50_Countries_500_Cities_SEO_Targets.xlsx`
  * `31_Services_x_10_Keywords.xlsx`
  * `src/data/cities.json`
  * `src/data/services.json`
